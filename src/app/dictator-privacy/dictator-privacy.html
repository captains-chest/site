Privacy policy for Dictator

Dictator for Android and iOS collects no data. Your voice and your text stay on your device.

Effective 15 October 2026 · Applies to Dictator for Android (com.captainduckay.dictator.android) and Dictator for iOS (com.captainduckay.dictator.ios), published by CaptainDuckay.

What we collect

Nothing. Dictator has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. No personal data is sent to us or to anyone else, and nothing is sold or shared.

The microphone

Dictator uses the microphone only while you are dictating, to turn your speech into text on the device with an on-device speech model. On Android you choose NVIDIA Parakeet or OpenAI Whisper on first launch; on iOS the app uses a bundled Whisper model. The recording is kept in memory only until it has been transcribed and is then discarded. It is never saved to storage and never leaves the device.

Offline by design

Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone. Audio and transcripts never leave the device. On Android, the chosen speech model is downloaded once after you confirm (Wi-Fi recommended); that download from Hugging Face is the only network use, and every file is checked against a SHA-256 manifest that ships inside the app. On iOS, the speech model is bundled with the app and the app does not use the network for dictation.

Your text

On Android, a floating microphone appears when a text field is focused. Dictator types the transcript into that field through an accessibility service. The service reads only the focused field (a little context before the cursor for spacing and capitals), stores nothing, and never uses the clipboard. On iOS, a custom keyboard shows the mic button; the finished transcript is passed from the app to the keyboard on the device and deleted as soon as it has been typed.

What is stored on the device

Your settings, such as the dictation language and (on Android) which speech model you chose, plus the downloaded speech model files on Android. Uninstalling the app removes them. Android backups of the app are turned off.

Children

Dictator collects no data from anyone, including children.
